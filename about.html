About Chai Corner

Bringing authentic street tea traditions to your table

Our Story

Founded with a passion for authentic tea brewing, Chai Corner brings the comforting vibe of traditional roadside dhabas straight to tea lovers. Every cup is brewed carefully using fresh ingredients, whole spices, and rich milk to give you an unforgettable taste.

🎯 Mission

To serve the finest authentic Karak Chai made with fresh spices and warmth.

⭐ Quality First

We source tea leaves directly from organic gardens and prepare everything fresh.
